# Screenshots

Copied from `pve-herdr-agents/design/screenshots/`. Three of the seven are here.

## Not copied, and why

Do not add these back without editing the source mockup first.

**`workspace.png`** — the agent feed in it answers a question with what read as real
internal issue tracker entries: a named project and three ticket ids with their titles and
statuses. That is not filler an outside reader would recognise as filler, and this is a public
page.

**`tab-diff.png`** — its two actions are `COPY PATCH` and `OPEN PR`. The product's actions are
push and discard, and opening a pull request is in the deliberately-not-built list
(`/docs/concepts/not-built`). Shipping it would advertise a feature that does not exist.

**`foundations.png`** — a design-system sheet, not a product shot. Nothing wrong with it, just
not marketing material.

**`tab-details.png`** — fine to use, simply not needed by the current landing page.

## Aspect ratios

`home.png` is 2880x1800. The `tab-*.png` are 800x1760. Do not mix the two in one grid — the wide
one renders unreadably small in a narrow column.
